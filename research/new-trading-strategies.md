# PMCC Strategy Research Index

Last updated: 2026-06-09

This file is intentionally not a mirror of the strategy register.

Canonical strategy artifacts:

- `docs/new-trading-strategies.md` is the strategy register.
- `docs/strategy-result-ledger.md` is the performance ledger.
- `research/trading-strategy-research.md` contains source-backed research notes, candidate strategies, and longer-form rationale.

Use this `research/` path for research discovery only. When a strategy changes status, update the canonical files under `docs/` and then link supporting research here if needed.
